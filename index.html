Sea Battle

Hunt the enemy fleet. Every ship brings its own weapon.

How to play

  1. Place your five ships. Tap a ship to pick it up, tap the water to drop it.
  2. Take turns firing at the enemy grid. Sink every enemy ship to win.
  3. Instead of firing, you can use one ship's ability. It then needs a few turns to recharge.
  4. Lose a ship and you lose its ability.
