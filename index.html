How to play

  1. Position your five ships. Tap a ship to pick it up, tap the water to drop it.
  2. Take turns firing at the enemy grid. Sink every enemy ship to win.
  3. Instead of firing, use one ship's weapon. It then needs a few turns to reload.
  4. Lose a ship and you lose its weapon.
